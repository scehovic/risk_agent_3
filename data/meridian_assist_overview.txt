MERIDIAN ASSIST — SECURITY AND ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
Prepared by Meridian Data Systems for customer due diligence
Document reference MDS-SEC-OV-114, revision 6, June 2026

1. PRODUCT SUMMARY

Meridian Assist is a multi-tenant software-as-a-service application that generates
suggested written replies for contact centre agents. Each customer organisation is
provisioned an isolated logical tenant within a shared platform. Assist is delivered from
Meridian's primary region in Dublin, Ireland, with a warm standby in Frankfurt, Germany.

2. HOSTING AND TENANCY

Tenant data is stored in a dedicated database schema per customer organisation. Tenants
share the underlying compute and storage fabric. Meridian does not offer single-tenant
deployment on the standard commercial terms.

3. ENCRYPTION AND KEY MANAGEMENT

All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Encryption keys are held in a
managed hardware security module operated by Meridian, are separated from the data they
protect, and are rotated automatically every ninety days. Keys are never written to
application configuration or to source control. Customer-managed keys are available on
the enterprise tier at additional cost and are not enabled by default.

Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3 on all external connections and TLS 1.2 or
above on connections internal to the platform.

4. EXTERNAL ATTACK SURFACE

Meridian publishes and maintains a current register of every internet-reachable endpoint
in the Assist platform. The register is reviewed monthly by the platform security team
and is available to customers on request under non-disclosure. The Assist platform
exposes exactly three internet-facing services: the agent console at
console.meridianassist.com, the tenant administration API, and the public status page.
Administrative interfaces other than the tenant administration API are not reachable from
the public internet and are accessible only from Meridian's corporate network.

An external penetration test is commissioned annually. The most recent test was completed
in March 2026 by an independent CREST-accredited assessor; the summary report is available
to customers on request.

5. SUPPORT MODEL

Tier 1 and tier 2 support are delivered from Meridian's service centre in Bengaluru,
India, operating across three shifts. Support engineers require administrative access to
a customer tenant in order to diagnose issues. Access is granted through Meridian's
standard support role and is exercised over the customer's site-to-site VPN where one is
configured.

6. LOGGING

The Assist platform records authentication events, configuration changes and API calls in
a central log store retained for thirteen months. Prompt and completion payloads are
retained for thirty days for quality purposes. Log export to a customer's own log
platform is available on the enterprise tier.

7. SUBPROCESSORS

Meridian maintains a published list of subprocessors at
meridiandatasystems.com/legal/subprocessors and provides thirty days' notice of any
addition or replacement.

8. MODEL

Assist uses a commercially licensed large language model accessed through Meridian's
inference layer. Meridian does not disclose the model provider, the model family or the
model version, and reserves the right to change the underlying model to maintain or
improve output quality. Customer prompts and completions are not used to train any model,
and are not shared with the model provider for any purpose other than serving the
request.

9. CERTIFICATION

Meridian Data Systems holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification and produces an annual SOC 2
Type II report. The current report covers the period to September 2025 and the Dublin and
Frankfurt hosting environments.
